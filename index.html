Frequently Asked Questions — ADVENTURE Travel 2026

What gear and equipment are required for wilderness expeditions?

All technical climbing equipment, tents, and safety ropes are provided by ADVENTURE. Participants are recommended to bring waterproof hiking boots, moisture-wicking layers, personal toiletries, and a 40L backpack.

How can I book an upcoming tour package with instant confirmation?

You can reserve your spot directly using our online booking form or message our expedition desk via the contact section below. All reservations come with 100% free cancellation up to 14 days before departure.

Are guided adventure tours suitable for beginners?

Yes! We offer beginner-friendly itineraries like the Coastal Rainforest Trek and Desert Safari, which require zero prior climbing experience and feature full certified guide support.
